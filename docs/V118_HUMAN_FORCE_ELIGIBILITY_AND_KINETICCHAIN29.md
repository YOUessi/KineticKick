# KineticKick v0.1.118 — real-human evidence qualification and executable KineticChain-29 acceptance design

Date: 2026-11-12. The real PhysX results do **not** demonstrate learned elite soccer biomechanics. The original G1 full-body controller was unchanged. This is a bounded source-eligibility assessment and a concrete design for the next genuinely new controller, not a new policy performance claim.

### Data admission levels

* **K0** untrusted visual or uncalibrated robot proxy: descriptive only; never supervision for expert impact or GRF.
* **K1** measured real full-body 3D kinematics with calibrated timestamps/marker labels, known subject/trial and missingness: may supervise **kinematic timing only** (CMU data meet *parts* of K1 after missing-label review).
* **K2** K1 + synchronized measured human feet force-plate GRF/CoP with foot assignment, support-switch and calibrated units: may supervise stance/support phase, but no real instep impact claim yet.
* **K3** K2 + synchronized boot/ball strike point and timed contact, measured ball impact/velocity, athlete performance metadata and source permissions: qualifies real human soccer impact learning and expert comparison only after full evidence audit.
* **K4** multi-player K3 independent train/val/test subjects, repeated boot-to-ball high-speed shot trials, contact/impulse uncertainty bounds and reproducible biomechanical evaluation: needed to **defend elite human-explosive claims**. The project has not reached K2–K4.

Qualification must include source hashes, physical units/frame, sample clock calibration, camera/plate calibration, dominant/support leg, kick phase annotations with uncertainty, real ball geometry and legible license. Uncertain labels must remain absent; no labels from synthetic robot impulses may be laundered into human truth.

## Physically executable new control design (future, not actuated in v118)

1. A common continuous human motion phase latent `z(t)` over **approach → stance foot planted → backswing → pelvis/torso acceleration → instep contact → follow-through → recovery**. No switching to a trivial 1mrad-only near-contact weak reference. Input relative human pelvis/shoulder/hip/swing leg/contralateral arm trajectories with mask and uncertainty, true chronological timestamps, plus robot foot contact, COM, original 29 q/dq, ball geometry, last action and **qualified native collider clearances**. Output a **jointly generated full-horizon 29DoF q*/dq*/tau* and stance mode** at 200Hz.
2. Learn temporally coupled *full-body* phase functions with a recurrent transformer or continuous-time latent and 29DoF whole-body retargeting. Human marker-relative phase velocity and cross-body delays influence the **pelvis–trunk–opposite arm–support leg–swing leg together**; measured human foot forces inform stance only if K2+. K1-only conditioning cannot enable elite contact loss, GRF loss or football ball impact loss.
3. Underlying motor interface must solve a constrained whole-body dynamic tracking/viability problem for torques and accelerations, not merely follow a kinematic target: original q, dq, ddq, actuator torque limits on all 29, active stance foot unilateral force/friction and slip, COM capture or centroidal momentum feasibility, signed self-collision clearance **with verified real collision paths**, and foot/ball legal instep at impact. Receding horizon extends through actual 14s landing/recovery.
4. Encode seven-phase feasibility and every-frame refusal in `tools/v117_witnessed_horizon_admission.py` and `v107_motor_admission.py`. Missing original native actor/collider witness, stance/slip/CoP or qualified human labels must **refuse**, even with mathematically safe numeric vectors. The v118 independent temporal audit code `v118_phase_power_chain_audit.py` and `v118_controller_earliest_effect.py` tests causal phase *observations*, not the proposed generator.
5. Freeze independent controls, random seeds, equipment and data split **before training**: previous v52–v118 robot physics failures and same-subject CMU all development/training eligibility only; genuinely unseen human subjects and robot geometry/seeds reserved for untouched final tests. No post hoc optimization on AM/AN because they are now observed. Compare v32/CMU, wholebody robot-only, K1 human conditioned, K2 stance-conditioned when qualified, arm-locked, pelvis-locked, shuffled real human time, stance-blind and full human + robot dynamics. Multiple independent seeds across ball offsets and directions. Report **per-scene paired deltas** and missing-evidence masks.
6. Naturalness primary metrics (where K3/K4 qualified): real trained expert 3-D pelvis–torso phase lag, contralateral arm momentum, support foot timing, stance-to-contact impulse and follow-through recovery measured continuously from kick onset, not joint MSE or just ball speed. Robot hard gates remain conjunction: legal instep share >=0.95, actual goal entry, original ball speed floor >=3.5m/s **not elite level**, every original q/dq/ddq/tau gate, no non-foot collision, no unsafe slip, actual post-contact and 14s stand. Never allow fast scored goals to hide qdot failures or unverified collider paths.

## v118 actual evidence: falsification and source constraints

* Two unseen robot geometries: AM 3/3 original controls strict PASS; AN 0/3 FAIL goal **despite ~5.42m/s proxy and 100% simulated instep contact share**. This is a spatial generalization failure, not learned motor improvement.
* First measurable old CMU vs v32 29DoF velocity difference on every pair at 1.405s, 0.160–0.165s before actual impact; no full approach causal support. Phase power-onset indicators are exactly the same across compared controls at each ball geometry; they are recorded robot motor power, not human joint torques.
* Native regular and full contact API subscriptions/polling returned 0 headers despite Tensor 113 contacts and 667N isolated positive in v118. Therefore zero G1 filtered Tensor force can **never** certify absence of left-wrist/left-hip collision. Actual native actor/collider geometry remains OPEN and new motor policy remains unactuated.

All empirical raw arrays remain ignored on Tang. The Git branch includes executable protocols, scanners, tests, reports. No inference of professional athlete data or certified self-collision clearance is made.
