# KineticKick v0.1.95 — 29DoF whole-body action candidates, human data qualification, and safety evidence levels

## Research objective (NOT accomplished)
A 29DoF Unitree G1 should learn expert-human run-up, support-foot establishment, pelvis and torso rotation, proximal-to-distal hip/knee/ankle swing, counter-arm timing, legal fast instep impact, follow-through, replanting, and independent standing recovery. The principal research hypothesis is that learned full-body temporal coordination increases perceived and kinematic human naturalness, not that a foot-only ball nudge raises scoring rate.

There are four separate levels of claims. Never silently promote a lower-level observation into a higher-level one:
1. Expert human kick kinematics with verified synchronized bilateral GRF/CoP and true instep ball-impact timing/impulse: **not qualified** in the six local CMU C3D snippets (v92); 0/6 under the explicitly retrospective strict >90%-marker-visibility rule and 0/6 verified expert instep-force cases. Same-subject clips dominate (5/6), and true human contact impulse/elite identity were not measured.
2. Measured robot original G1 motion and contact under real 200Hz Isaac Sim PhysX: yes; historical original 29DoF v91 episodes E/F 0/4 strict physically successful, with 2/4 censored at ~1.6s; robot observations are NOT human demonstrations.
3. The current trainable output: actual full29, 0.4s, 50Hz velocity sequences, integrated from current measured positions into 29D joint position proposals, from a multi-output ridge basis of six temporal knots. True trained robot-only qdot imitation; **not** verified human expert imitation; no motor dispatch, no online inverse dynamics, no guarantee that position, motor torque, friction or self-clearance remains safe.
4. Future learned expert human imitation with torque-limited, stance-aware and shape-calibrated G1 physical closed-loop actuation, never seen physical seeds and ball positions, plus strict impact, safety and recovery: **blocked**.

## What is actually implemented
Source: tools/v095_trajectory_generator.py; tests/test_v095_trajectory_generator.py.

Training sources: original exact-SHA original G1 v84 scenes C/D (C_v32_off, C_cmu02, D_v32, D_cmu02). Retrospective holdout: original v91 E/F (E_v32, E_cmu02, F_v32, F_cmu02). These E/F episodes have already been examined in past versions, so this is an **exploratory cross-scene diagnostic** rather than a pristine pre-registered paper test. Data are read-only and original PhysX proof is tied to each case's local SHA record.

At each t, inputs are measured current q(29), dq(29)/original 29 speed limits, COM and ball positions relative to pelvis/root, kick-foot velocity and causal observed current stance ground loads. No future GRF/contact time/ball impulse or future motor target enters features. A multioutput alpha=10 ridge maps to 6 anchors across twenty 50Hz future samples; interpolation produces a real 20 x 29 normalized qdot candidate; integration with the source robot's measured 29 speed limits and current q(t) generates a full 20 x 29 q candidate. Velocity and resulting joint positions are **proposals**, not dynamic-control commands. Conversion does NOT check joint angle range, acceleration, torque, friction-cone, actual Jacobians or collision distance. Filter v94 will fail closed if genuine original native pair/collider evidence is not supplied and is itself offline-only. Do not bypass.

Ablations are actually emitted sequences, not pretend training: state-only, state+current support, shuffled-support training negative control, reverse future-time order, freeze 14 arm joints, keep only right strike leg. Group partition covers all 29 original joint names: left stance leg 6, right striking leg 6, waist/trunk 3, left counterarm 7, right arm 7. Experimental score is **normalized predicted joint velocity error against original robot motion**, not human action naturalness, measured success at shooting, or learned expert torque.

Out-of-distribution results must show negative results too. Examples of heldout normalized all29 qdot MAE (lower is closer to original *robot* trajectory, not necessarily more natural): E_v32 state=0.030449, state+stance=0.030426, reversed=0.074143, frozen arms=0.040220, right-leg-only=0.047419; E_cmu02 state=0.017052, state+stance=0.017015, reversed=0.028365; F_v32 state=0.026321, state+stance=0.026042, reversed=0.092582. The F_v32 and F_cmu02 physical sources end early with strongly correlated traces, do not pretend they provide independent recovery validation. Current contact-conditioning error improvement is minor, and some group phase metrics worsen. Thus there is **no evidence yet** of expert-like naturalness, power or generative superiority.

## Contact positive control and original G1 fail-closed condition
v95 ran nine isolated independent original Isaac Sim 4.5/PhysX *sphere-box*, 180 true steps each (5ms). CUDA tensor contact filtered pair positive in two independent cases and 0 for separated negative. Native subscribed before/after simulation.reset, with/without tensor, yielded zero native actor/collider event in these positive-collision scenes, so it remains **uncalibrated**. USD collider inventory is not the true point-level native pair/manifold. Only six OFF/ON exact comparisons of **saved sphere z/vz and box position** passed; equality of all original G1 physical state is NOT proven by these isolated calibration runs. Original G1 true wrist--hip self-collision is still a hypothesis based on opposite *net link forces*, and not native shape proof. Do not dispatch the new generator to original G1 motors.

## Candidate next-stage method and preregistration, not yet executed
Human data eligibility must pass verified license/download provenance, >=2 genuinely different subject identities with a subject-disjoint split, a time-synchronized frame-by-frame source, a professional or at least independently validated instep action with real ball and contact time, and ideally measured foot GRF/CoP. Do not falsely require perfect GRF to learn kinematic style, but mark GRF-absent style prior and treat physical impact as **robot-side only**. Use a public support-force dataset solely for support estimation if kick data remain unavailable.

Research promising sources (not qualified as professional kick impact):
- CMU Soccer Mocap, subject 10/11; https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/ — local six C3Ds lack synced ground forces and certified instep ball impulse.
- GroundLink, https://csr.bu.edu/groundlink/ and https://hanxingjian.github.io/groundlink.html — synchronized human poses with measured left/right GRF/CoP for general movement; no verified expert instep kick impulse in the audited source. Check license and training splits before downloading.
- SportsPose https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01865 — sports pose diversity, not synchronized force/ball impact; geometry-only research.

A later independently frozen *new version* should introduce expert-conditioned 29DoF generator (phase features run-up/plant/pelvis/trunk/swing/counterarm/impact/follow-through/replant), kinematic retarget with original URDF and correct contact times, physically calibrated shape pair distance, support/Jacobian constraints, torque and qdot limits, and adversarial negative examples. Frozen arms, foot-only, reversed-phase, no-support and robot-only baselines are mandatory. Train ONLY on approved sources; reserve unseen human subjects, ball sites, friction settings and random resets. Separate motion naturalness via event-phase time lags, independent blinded reviewers and stance metrics from goal/contact/speed/safety hard-gate outcomes.

Physical gate for a **future motor-driven original G1 trial**: true >=95% instep impulse share, legal goal, ball-speed >=3.5m/s *minimum* (far from elite power), full measured original29 qdot within bounds, nonfoot force <20N and no forbidden hard collision, full postimpact >=1.25s and 14s upright recovery. Never call a partially recovered shot successful and never claim an elite-human generation method from the v95 offline fit.
