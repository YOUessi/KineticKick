# KineticKick v0.1.97: paper hypothesis and honest go/no-go on human kick learning

**Scientific target:** transfer from *qualified real human football performance* the time-coupled full-body balance/kinetic chain of approach, planted left support leg, pelvis yaw and tilt, lumbar/trunk angular motion, right hip extension/swing leg, contralateral left arm swing, foot instep contact, follow-through, landing and 14s stable recovery, to a **freely balanced** original 29DoF G1. A lawful kick with goal and speed is a required consequence, not the primary contribution or a substitute for stylistic naturalness.

**Current no-go:** v97 proved 3/6 *existing* frozen G1 PhysX shots strict PASS at *fixed* G/H ball placements, not a learned policy. G's all-three failures show contact legality ~70% instep and recovery weakness. Native original-G1 direct collider manifold and a high-quality elite human kick with synchronized GRF and actual ball contact impulse remain missing. Robot-only full29 future ridge produces original URDF angle violations in many G/H windows despite normalized speed <=1. **Do not authorize actuation** of any candidate.

## Required supervised data contract before claiming human imitation
- License/source exact and immutable recording SHA; documented professional-football/representative high-quality provenance, appropriate shot styles and sequence diversity (different people, speed and support side).
- Actual time-synchronized human full-body 3D kinematics (joint positions/orientations, world scale, calibration, actor identity), timing of approach/planted support/backswing/impact/follow-through/recovery. If feet are optical proxies, label the uncertainty and refuse to call the optical event true impact time.
- Actual measured left/right foot GRF/CoP and reliable foot-ball point/normal/impulse (or explicitly separate datasets without these); no inferred GRF or elite labels fabricated from CMU 120Hz 10_02/10_05 C3D.
- Split by player and recording session, freeze held-out athletes/fields/ball placements and adversarial collision/early termination trials **before tuning**.
- Report hardware/physics reproducer, retargeting frame basis and joint-name order, human-to-G1 scale mapping, missing values, timing deltas and label provenance.

## Proposed method (not yet demonstrated or deployed)

1. A **joint time-course conditional latent generator** encodes support phase and contact time distribution, full six-chain features (left stance leg/pelvis/waist/right kicking leg/left arm/right arm), root COM/pelvis velocity and future landing objective; predicts **whole 29DoF trajectories and root motion**, not ankle-only residual nor short joint-position cosine MSE.
2. A differentiable retargeter preserves human proximal-distal **temporal order**, relative angular momentum and counterarm–pelvis lag. Train with qualified human 120/200Hz motion windows where supported; express uncertainty where human force data absent. The only true human force/impact loss terms may use verified synchronized force/ball signals.
3. **Original G1 projection before every 5ms actuation**: enforce all real 29 joint angles and velocities, joint accelerations, effort/torque via robot inverse dynamics, planted foot 3D Jacobian slip, friction cone and measured bilateral GRF/CoP, signed per-collider distance for wrist/hip and pelvis/hip, phase-sensitive full-body momentum, ball contact normal aimed at correct instep, goal, and actual 14s recovery.
4. A *positive-native-contact-report calibrated* PhysX contact channel and an off/on identical-state control must prove instrumentation noninterference on actual original G1. Empty callbacks are UNKNOWN; opposite net forces are not manifold/shape ID proof. Reject a candidate if any certificate is missing, stale, contradictory, or solver infeasible.
5. **Independent blinded physical factorial**: held-out *people* for biological naturalness, held-out ball places/initial states + seeded real root/joint perturbations for G1, baseline original v32, human-trained wholebody, frozen arms, wrong phase, foot-only, robot-only prediction, and optional synthetic controls. Score all 29 measured qdot, strict >=95% legal instep, contact force/ball speed/goal, self-collision/contact, support margin, foot-ground slip, effort, follow-through and stable recovery **conjunctively**. Preserve ALL failures.

**v97 falsification anchor:** original physical six-place outcomes G all FAIL and H all PASS, no proven human-style benefit; robot-only 29DoF proposals frequently violate original URDF angles even though qdot passes. This shows why a learned full-body 20-step trajectory predictor is not the research endpoint or a safety-certified controller.

Do not describe G/H results as statistically independent player samples, or the 3/6 original-controller success as learned-policy shooting success. Native actor/collider manifold count remains zero; professional-impact transfer and physical new controller rollout remain zero.
